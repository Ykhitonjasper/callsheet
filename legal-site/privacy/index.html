Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

This policy explains how CallSheet (“the app”) handles information when you use it on iPhone.

Information we collect

CallSheet does not require an account and does not upload your call notes, quotes, or blocks to a server. We do not operate an app backend.

Blocks, slips, and settings stay on this iPhone. The Call button opens Apple’s Phone app with the number stored on that slip. CallSheet does not identify unknown callers and does not read your call history. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device access

The app does not request access to your location, camera, photo library, microphone, or contacts. Placing a call hands the number to the system Phone app.

Information that may leave your device

Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads this website from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver the page. CallSheet does not use that visit to identify you inside the app.

The support form opens your email app. Your email provider sends the message to our support address. The website does not store a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you use Delete All Data in Settings or uninstall the app. We cannot restore it.

Children

CallSheet is a general-audience utility. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
